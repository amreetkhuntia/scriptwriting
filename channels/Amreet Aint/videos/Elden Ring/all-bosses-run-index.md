## Verification method

The channel's numbered uploads were enumerated with `yt-dlp`. Available Hindi/English captions were searched for candidate moments, then each candidate was checked against frames from a narrowly downloaded video range. Episodes without usable captions (#5, #6, #9, #18, and #20) were located and checked visually.
